Lesson 030: forward vs deferred vs Forward+

    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    What this shows
    
    
    
      
    Cost is lights evaluated
    Each pixel pays for every light on its list, whether it reaches or not. Forward with limit 8 evaluates 6 lights per pixel for 1.1 that really reach.
    
      
    Wrong can look fast
    Forward with limit 4 is fast because it skips 62% of the light. The "Light missing" view shows what the GPU time hides.
    
      
    Every renderer has a second bill
    Deferred pays in G-buffer bandwidth and memory, and its water still uses forward. Forward+ pays a light-assignment pass and over-counts a little per tile.
    
    
    
    
    The light lists, tiles and evaluation counts are computed for real by this page on a 160 × 72 grid (one square = 10 × 10 screen pixels), in 2D: real clustered renderers also cut tiles by depth, and real engines sort lights by intensity, not only distance. The costs are the lesson's simulation: 12 ms of pixel work without lanterns, 2 ms per light evaluated on every pixel, 0.02 ms and 10 µs of CPU per deferred light, +12 ms of memory work for a G-buffer in memory (+1.5 on chip), vertex 8, raster 4, memory 14, 3 ms fixed.
