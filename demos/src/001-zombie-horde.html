    What this shows

    O(n²) hides in small tests

    With 500 zombies, checking everyone looks free. Each time you double the crowd, the work goes up four times, so the cost shows up suddenly.

    Measure, then fix

    The frame bar is the profiler in miniature. The separation step is the one thing to fix; the rest of the frame barely changes.

    The architect's rule

    Any "who is near me?" question goes through one shared spatial grid. Then bullets, explosions and AI senses get the same speed-up for free.

    Times are measured in JavaScript in your browser. A C# or C++ build will give different absolute numbers, but the same shape: about ×4 per doubling for "check everyone" and about ×2 for the grid. The frame rate cannot go above your screen's refresh rate (often 60 or 120 Hz).
