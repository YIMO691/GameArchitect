Lesson 015: singletons, service locator, dependency injection

      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    What this shows
    
    
    
      
    Singletons are fast, not safe
    A static field costs about 1 ns. The bill comes elsewhere: helpers you can't see, a start-up order left to luck, and tests that need the whole Reef.
    
      
    A phone book is not a fix
    A locator lets tests use fakes, but the helpers stay hidden, a missing fake crashes at run time, and asking inside a loop costs 40 ns per question.
    
      
    Injection moves the wiring to one place
    Constructors list every helper. The container builds them in dependency order. A forgotten fake is a compile error. The price: about 2 ms at load and setup code.
    
    
    
    
    This lab is a simulation, not a measurement. Costs come from Lesson 015 and are approximate phone numbers: a static or cached field read 1 ns; a locator Get<T>() 40 ns (dictionary lookup 35 + cast 5); 3 helper uses per gull per step; the rest of the critter step fixed at 0.33 ms; a PlayMode test that loads the Reef 6 s; a test with fakes 0.5 ms; a container build of 2 ms at load. The 1-in-25 bad start stands for an Awake order Unity doesn't promise; on a real device it depends on the build and the scene.
