Lesson 052: audio budgets

    Lesson 056 · Challenge: predict first, then tap Set up and check

      What this shows

      Audio has a hard deadline

      The sound card needs a new buffer every 10.7 ms. Miss it once and the player hears a click. Unlike a slow frame, it can't be hidden.

      Ears can't count to 180

      Past a few dozen sounds, nobody hears the difference. A voice cap with priorities keeps what matters and drops the rest.

      Choose loading per sound

      Long music streams from disk; short, frequent effects stay in memory in a cheap format. One setting for everything wastes either memory or CPU.

      This lab is a simulation, not a measurement. It uses Lesson 052's numbers: 180 requests in the finale (1 music, 1 reel loop, 1 announcer, 120 bangs, 40 splashes, 17 cheers); a 10.7 ms buffer (512 samples at 48 kHz) and a 5 ms budget; 0.4 ms mixing overhead; per voice 0.06 ms decompressed, 0.07 ms ADPCM, 0.15 ms Vorbis, 0.2 ms for a streamed track; memory 220 MB decompressed (music 207.4, effects 12.6), 22 MB all Vorbis, 4 MB with streamed music and ADPCM effects; 5,600 buffers a minute.
