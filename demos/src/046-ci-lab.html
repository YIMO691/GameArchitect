Lesson 046: build pipelines and CI

    What this shows

    Fast and narrow first

    A 6-minute check on every commit finds most breaks, and the suspect is always one commit: the one just pushed.

    Build time decides the batch

    A 160-minute build can only test 13 commits at a time. A cached 25-minute build tests 2. Fewer suspects, faster fixes.

    Some breaks only show on the device

    A crash at launch passes every compile and test. Only running the build on a phone finds it before people do.

    This lab is a simulation, not a measurement. It uses Lesson 046's numbers: 38 commits in an 8-hour day (4.75 an hour); breaks a day: 1.2 compile or test, 0.5 Android-only, 0.2 crash at launch; a nightly clean build of 160 min whose breaks are seen the next morning, 9 working hours later on average; fast checks 6 min; an Android build back to back on one machine; the smoke test 10 min after it.
