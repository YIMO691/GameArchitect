Frame Pipeline Lab

The CPU and the GPU work at the same time, one frame apart. The slower one sets your frame rate. Change the work on each side and watch who ends up waiting.

Night city level. Goal: 60 fps on a 60 Hz screen at 100% render scale, with at least 1.5 ms of headroom.

    What this shows
    
    
    
      
    Frame = the slower side
    Frame time is max(CPU, GPU). Saving time on the faster side turns into waiting, not into frames.
    
      
    Waiting looks like work
    The main thread's total includes the wait for the GPU. Read the wait marker before you believe "CPU 25 ms".
    
      
    The bottleneck moves
    After every big fix, check again which side is slower. The 50% test and undoing the CPU work both flip it.
    
    
    
    
    This lab is a model, not a measurement. Pass costs come from Lesson 002: fixed GPU work 5 ms (shadows 3 ms, 2 ms of geometry) and per-pixel work 20 ms at 2560×1440. Per-pixel work scales with the number of pixels, so 50% render scale means ¼ of the cost. Real GPUs are less tidy, but the rules are the same.
