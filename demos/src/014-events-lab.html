    What this shows
    
    
    
      
    The call is cheap, the place is not
    In direct calls and both buses, 900 of every catch's nanoseconds are cold reaction work. Only the queue makes it warm, by doing 600 of the same job in a row.
    
      
    Fewer wires, new costs
    A bus cuts 15 wires to 8. An object bus pays with boxing, list copies and garbage; every bus pays with nested cascades and a surprising order.
    
      
    Late is fine for news, not for decisions
    A queue delays reactions. A score number one step late is invisible. A fish that two gulls both catch is a bug: claim it now, tell others later.
    
    
    
    
    This lab is a simulation, not a measurement. Costs come from Lesson 014 and are approximate phone numbers: cold reaction 90 ns, warm 15 ns; direct call 2 ns; object bus 120 ns per publish + 10 ns per handler and 88 B of garbage; typed bus 15 + 5 ns; queue 3 ns per append + 4 ns per read. Critter systems cost 0.21 ms (Lesson 013), the heap has 8 MB of free room, and a GC stop costs 12 ms. Real engines vary, but the shape is the same.
