Lesson 047: save systems and versioning

    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    What this shows
    
    
    
      
    Silent loss is the worst loss
    Without a version, the loader can't tell an old save from a broken one. It fills in defaults, and the next autosave makes the loss permanent.
    
      
    Migrations need old saves to test
    Each step of the chain is small. The bugs hide in saves your team stopped making months ago. Keep real ones and load them in CI.
    
      
    A save is a transaction
    Write the new file next to the old one, then swap. A crash in the middle leaves the old save, not half of each.
    
    
    
    
    This lab is a simulation, not a measurement. It uses Lesson 047's numbers: 200,000 players whose last save is from 1.0 (20,000), 1.1 (24,000), 1.2 (46,000) or 1.3 (110,000); 30,000 of them own boats, 3,600 of those with a 1.1 save, where boats were stored as a list of ids; 10 saves a player a day, and 1 save in 25,000 interrupted by the phone closing the app.
