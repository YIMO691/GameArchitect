Lesson 064: crash analysis

    What this shows

    A few causes are most of the crashes

    The top 8 causes are 69% of all crashes. But you only see that when each cause is one row on the dashboard.

    Symbols and grouping make the rows true

    Without symbols, one crash is a different row in every build. Whole-stack grouping splits it again by every path into the allocator.

    Context makes a crash fixable

    "Out of memory" alone is a shrug. "Out of memory, 2 GB phones, after the third lake, 1,280 MB used" is a ticket.

    This lab is a model, not real crash data. It uses Lesson 064's numbers: 46 real causes, the biggest 22% of crashes and the top 10 together 72.5%; 3 builds live at once; a cause reaches the stack's top frame by 1 to 12 different paths. Three of the top causes (out of memory, the GPU driver crash, a streaming race) can only be fixed with context. Fixing a cause removes all its rows. A pick that turns out to be a cause already being fixed is a duplicate.
