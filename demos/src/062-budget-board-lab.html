Lesson 062: budgets as a team process

    What this shows

    A budget without an owner is a wish

    When the total is everybody's, regressions wait for the optimizer team, who don't know the code and can't keep up.

    Old regressions cost more

    A regression found the day it lands takes 2 hours from the person who wrote it. Found a month later, it takes hours of searching through everything since.

    Alert first, block with care

    A CI alert to a named owner keeps releases in budget. Blocking every merge is safer still, but every regression then delays a feature.

    This lab is a model, not a measurement. It uses Lesson 062's numbers: at launch the frame is 14.3 ms against a 16.0 ms budget (16.7 ms is a dropped frame at 60 fps); one regression lands each week (0.3–0.7 ms). The optimizer team has 32 hours a month and takes 4 h + 4 h per week of age to fix one (4 h if CI already named the change). An owner takes 2 h + 2 h per week of age. In weeks 2, 6 and 10 (live events) owners are too busy and fix the next week. Blocking costs the owner 2 h and the feature 1 day (3 days if the optimizer team must help).
