Lesson 055: telemetry and live ops

    What this shows

    The radio is the cost

    On a phone, waking the radio costs more than the bytes. Send events in batches, not one at a time.

    Design events for questions

    Log lines are for programmers. Typed events with a schema are for answering "why": funnels, cohorts and comparisons in one query.

    Change safely, without a build

    Remote config turns a two-week store update into minutes. Staged rollouts make sure a mistake reaches a few players, not all of them.

    This lab is a simulation, not a measurement. It uses Lesson 055's numbers: 400 events a player-hour; one request per event costs 6% battery an hour and 480 KB (1.2 KB each), batches every 60 s cost 0.8% and 90 KB; answering "why do day-2 players quit?" takes 216 hours (9 days) from log lines and 12 hours from typed events; a price change takes 336 hours (14 days) through the store and 0.5 hours with remote config; a bad change reaches 100,000 players at once or 10,000 in a 10% rollout.
