Lesson 054: tools and editor architecture

    What this shows

    Content is data

    When a new fish is a code change, every idea waits for a programmer. When it's a data asset with a good inspector, designers ship it themselves.

    Data needs its own compiler

    Moving content into data moves bugs into data too. Validators are the compiler for designers: they catch the typo on save, not in the play test.

    Tools have performance budgets

    A 40 s editor load is paid dozens of times a day by every designer. Tool speed is team speed.

    This lab is a simulation, not a measurement. It uses Lesson 054's numbers: 5 designers, 5 days; the editor opened 12 times a day each, 40 s for the whole lake or 3 s for one region; 6 new or changed fish a week, each costing a designer 2 h of waiting and a programmer 1.5 h when fish are code; the lake layout changes once a month, 2,400 spots at 1.5 min each by hand, or rules plus 240 hand tweaks; data bugs reaching builds: 14 a week with code, 20 with unchecked data, 1 with validators.
